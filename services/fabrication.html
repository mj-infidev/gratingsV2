Fabrication

Precision Grating Fabrication — Cut-to-Size Panels, Custom Cut-Outs & Complete Assembly Solutions

Precision Fabrication Solutions

Our fabrication facility combines advanced machinery, skilled workmanship, and strict quality control to deliver grating panels and components with excellent strength, accuracy, and surface finish. We fabricate and supply grating panels tailored to exact project and site requirements, ensuring precise fit and efficient installation.

All fabricated panels are finished with welded binding bars on all cut edges, using binding bars of the same size and thickness as the load-bearing bars. This provides enhanced structural strength, improved safety, and a clean, finished appearance.

Fabrication Capabilities

Rectangular Panels
Circular Cut-Outs
Combi Deck Systems
Industrial Stairs

Fabricated Rectangular Grating Panels

Rectangular steel grating panels cut to size as per approved drawings or site dimensions, with welded binding bars on all cut edges. Suitable for industrial walkways, platforms, flooring, trench covers, mezzanines, and access areas. Available in carbon steel, galvanized steel, or stainless steel.

Grating Panels with Cut-Outs

Rectangular steel grating panels with cut-outs designed to accommodate pipes, columns, ducts, equipment, and structural obstructions. All cut-out openings are reinforced with welded banding bars or toe-plates, providing additional strength, safety, and load continuity around the opening while maintaining structural integrity.

Stainless Steel Fabrication

Precision stainless steel fabrication solutions for commercial, industrial, and infrastructure projects. Our capabilities include cutting, bending, forming, welding, assembly, machining, and finishing — all using AISI 304/304L and 316/316L grade stainless steel. We also specialize in custom framed panels with edge angles and stiffeners.

Sheet Metal & Laser Cutting

Precision sheet metal fabrication up to 5 mm thickness ensuring accurate forming, cutting, and assembly. Our laser cutting capability supports material thicknesses up to 10 mm, delivering clean edges, tight tolerances, and consistent quality. Suitable for stainless steel, carbon steel, and other metals.

Combi Deck Grating with Chequered Plate

Closed open-bar steel grating with a 3 mm thick chequered plate either welded to the top surface or loose bolted, providing enhanced load-bearing capacity, additional strength, safety, and a non-slip surface. Ideal for industrial walkways, platforms, mezzanines, and heavy-duty flooring.

Fixing & Accessories

We supply complete fixing solutions alongside our fabricated panels for a secure, vibration-resistant installation:

  • Universal Grating Clips — No drilling or welding required. Fast top-side installation with standard hand tools.
  • M-Type Saddle Clips — Features a pressed bottom clamp and slotted hex bolt for maximum vibration resistance.
  • High-Grade Fasteners — Available in Mild Steel, Carbon Steel, and Stainless Steel (AISI 304/316) for all environments.
  • Versatile Application — Compatible with grating heights from 25 mm up to 60 mm, ensuring dependable performance.
