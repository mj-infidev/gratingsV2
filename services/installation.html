Installation

Complete On-Site Installation of Grating Systems, Handrails, Ladders & Access Solutions

Professional Grating Installation

Our experienced installation team provides complete on-site grating, handrail, ladder, and access system installation across Saudi Arabia and the GCC. Every installation follows approved drawings and project specifications, with secure fixing, precise alignment, and strict adherence to safety standards.

From initial site survey to final handover, we ensure that every panel is installed with proper clips, fasteners, and support structures to deliver a safe, secure, and long-lasting installation. Our team is equipped to handle EPC projects and industrial facilities.

Our Installation Process

01

Measurement & assessment as per approved drawings to ensure perfect fit and alignment on site.

02

Industrial Grating Panels

Installation of press-welded, swaged, press-locked, and FRP/GRP grating panels for walkways, platforms, trenches, mezzanines, and elevated flooring. All panels are secured with approved fixing clips and fasteners to ensure stability, safety, and vibration resistance in industrial environments.

Handrail & Guardrail Systems

Complete installation of tube-type handrail and guardrail systems meeting BS EN 14122-3 / ISO 14122-3 safety standards. Standard configurations include top rail, mid rail, and toe plate with knee rail. High-end stainless steel handrail systems installed with ball fittings for commercial and architectural projects.

Industrial Ladders & Stair Treads

Installation of side-step and through-access vertical caged ladders, step-over ladders, and grating stair treads for safe vertical access in industrial facilities. All ladders comply with EN ISO 14122-4 and EN 14122-2. Caged ladders include safety hoops at 600 mm intervals with rest platforms for height above 6 metres.

Access Covers & Manhole Systems

Installation of hinged access hatches, floor doors, and manhole covers for safe inspection and maintenance access. We install both standard grating access covers and heavy-duty cast/ductile iron covers compliant with BS EN 124 for vehicular load (Group 4 – D400 – 40 Ton).
