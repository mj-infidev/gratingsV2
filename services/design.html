Design & Engineering

Precision-Engineered Grating Solutions — Custom-Designed to Meet Your Project's Load, Span & Environmental Requirements

Engineering Excellence

At Gratings, our design and engineering team works at the forefront of grating technology — transforming project requirements into precisely engineered solutions. Every grating system we deliver is custom-designed to meet specific load, span, material, and environmental conditions, ensuring structural reliability and long-term performance.

From standard industrial platforms to complex architectural facades, our engineering capabilities cover the full spectrum of grating applications. We work closely with contractors, consultants, and project owners from initial concept through final delivery, ensuring on-time execution and dependable performance.

Our design philosophy is simple: build safer projects with uncompromising quality — engineered specifically for the demanding conditions of Saudi Arabia and the GCC region.

Our Design Process

01
Requirement Analysis

Understanding project loads, spans, environmental conditions, and safety standards to define the optimal grating specification.

02
Material Selection

Selecting the right material grade — Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel, Aluminium, or FRP — based on corrosion exposure and mechanical needs.

03
Engineering & Drafting

Producing detailed shop drawings, load calculations, and panel layouts with precise tolerances for consistent fit and finish.

04
Production & QC

Manufacturing under strict quality control with dimensional accuracy checks, ensuring every panel meets performance criteria.

What We Design

Our engineering team designs and specifies complete grating systems across the following product lines:

Steel Grating Systems

Press welded steel grating panels in standard and fabricated configurations — including rectangular panels, panels with cut-outs for pipes and columns, and combi deck gratings with chequered plate. Available in bearing bar depths from 20 mm to 100 mm, with plain or serrated surfaces.

Aluminium Grating Systems

Engineered for lightweight and corrosion-resistant applications — Flat Bar Gratings, Swaged Gratings (no welding), and T & I Bar Gratings. Manufactured from premium aluminium alloys EN AW 6060, 6063 T6, and 6082 T6 with bearing bar heights from 20 mm to 50 mm.

Stainless Steel Grating Systems

Designed for applications where structural reliability, corrosion resistance, and clean appearance are essential — Press-Locked, Pass-Through, Welded, and Wedge Wire configurations in AISI 304/304L and 316/316L grades. Bearing bar sizes up to 150 mm depth.

FRP / GRP Systems

Fiberglass reinforced plastic gratings engineered for corrosive, chemical, and high-humidity environments — Moulded Gratings, Pultruded Gratings, FRP Handrails, FRP Ladders, Cable Trays, and GRP Access Solutions.

Access & Safety Systems

Complete engineering for Handrail Systems (Welded, Stanchion-Type, and Modular), Industrial Ladders (with/without safety cage), Access Hatch Covers, Manhole Steps, and Stainless Steel Manhole Covers certified to BS EN 124.
