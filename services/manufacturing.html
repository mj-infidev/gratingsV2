Manufacturing

Precision Manufacturing of Industrial Grating Systems — Engineered for Strength, Durability & Long Service Life

Advanced Grating Manufacturing

Our manufacturing facility is equipped with advanced machinery and automated production systems to deliver high-quality grating panels with consistent dimensional accuracy and structural integrity. From raw material processing to final quality inspection, every step is controlled to meet international manufacturing standards.

We manufacture press welded steel gratings, hydraulically swaged aluminium gratings, press-locked stainless steel gratings, and FRP/GRP moulded and pultruded gratings — all under one roof. Our production capacity supports both standard panel manufacturing and custom-engineered solutions for EPC and mega projects across Saudi Arabia and the GCC.

Press Welding — Steel Gratings

Our automated press welding lines produce high-strength steel grating panels by electrically resistance-welding cross bars to load-bearing bars under high pressure. This process ensures a permanent fusion bond at every intersection, delivering panels with superior structural integrity. Standard panels are manufactured in 1000 mm × 6000 mm dimensions with bearing bar depths from 20 mm to 100 mm.

Hydraulic Swaging — Aluminium Gratings

Aluminium swaged gratings are manufactured without welding. Cross bars are hydraulically swaged under high pressure into pre-punched load bars, creating a permanent mechanical bond. This advanced process eliminates heat-affected zones and weld failures, ensuring exceptional structural integrity, uniform appearance, and long-term durability. Standard panels available in 6000 × 1000 mm and 3000 × 1000 mm.

Press Locking — Stainless Steel Gratings

Press-locked gratings are produced using precision-cut flat bars assembled through a high-pressure locking process. The bearing bars are accurately slotted, allowing cross bars to be mechanically pressed into position, creating a rigid, maintenance-free panel without welding. This method provides excellent dimensional accuracy and allows complete freedom in grating design with bearing bar sizes up to 150 mm depth.

Moulding & Pultrusion — FRP / GRP Gratings

FRP moulded gratings are manufactured in a single moulding process using continuous pre-tensioned glass fiber roving strands, thoroughly wetted with chemical resistant resin systems. Pultruded gratings use a continuous manufacturing process where glass fibers, reinforcement mats, and surface veils are drawn through a resin bath and shaped under controlled heat. Both produce high-performance structural elements with up to 70% fiberglass reinforcement.
