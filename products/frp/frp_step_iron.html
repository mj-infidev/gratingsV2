Product Overview

Manhole Step / Step Iron – Heavy Duty Access Solution

Engineered Safety for Underground Access Systems

Manhole Steps (Step Irons) are precision-engineered access components designed to provide safe, stable, and long-lasting entry and exit for personnel working in manholes, sewer systems, tanks, and underground chambers.

Manufactured from ductile iron core with high-performance polypropylene encapsulation or alternative metallic grades, these step irons deliver exceptional mechanical strength, corrosion resistance, and anti-slip performance in the most demanding environments.

The ergonomic anti-slip tread design, combined with side slip resistance geometry, ensures maximum safety even in wet, oily, or confined conditions.

Applications

Manhole Steps are designed for safe and reliable access in concrete, brick, steel, and composite structures across:

  • Sewer Manholes
  • Stormwater Drainage Systems
  • Water & Wastewater Treatment Plants
  • Underground Tanks & Reservoirs
  • Industrial & Chemical Facilities
  • Marine & Coastal Structures
  • Utility Chambers

Suitable for factories, mines, ships, lorries, swimming pools, and more.

Features

  • High Impact Durability – Engineered to withstand heavy loads and harsh use.
  • Wide Temperature Range – Reliable performance from −40°C to +120°C, suitable for extreme environments.
  • Excellent Electrical Insulation – Ensures safety in electrical and industrial applications.
  • Precision-Moulded & Dimensionally Stable – Maintains shape and fit over time.
  • Low Moisture Absorption – Prevents corrosion and maintains strength even in humid conditions.
  • Weather-Resistant – Designed to perform in outdoor and exposed environments.
  • Self-Extinguishing Material – Offers enhanced safety against fire hazards.
  • Clear Visibility & Safety Yellow Finish – Easy to spot and adds a layer of safety.
  • Non-Slip Design – Tread pattern prevents foot slippage, with side resistance for added security.
  • Maintenance-Free – No painting or additional coatings required, ready for immediate use.
