FRP Cable Tray

FRP / GRP Cable Tray Systems – Corrosion-Free Cable Management for Harsh Industrial Environments

Our FRP / GRP Cable Tray Systems are engineered using advanced pultrusion technology with high-performance, fire-retardant resin systems. Designed for long-term reliability, these systems provide a superior alternative to conventional steel trays, especially in highly corrosive, coastal, chemical, and high-humidity environments across Saudi Arabia and the GCC.

Built with high glass fiber content and thermosetting resins, FRP cable trays offer exceptional strength, lightweight handling, and complete resistance to rust, chemicals, and environmental degradation.

Resin Systems & Fire Performance

Resin Type Resin Base Performance Description Fire Rating (ASTM E84)
IFR Isophthalic Polyester (Fire Retardant) Industrial-grade resin combining corrosion resistance with fire-retardant properties. Suitable for general industrial and infrastructure applications. Class 1 (Flame Spread ≤ 25)
I Isophthalic Polyester Cost-effective solution with good resistance to mild chemicals and acidic environments. Recommended where fire performance is not required. Non Fire Retardant
VE Vinyl Ester Premium resin offering superior resistance to aggressive chemicals, acids, and solvents. Ideal for petrochemical and marine environments. Class 1 (Flame Spread ≤ 25)

Key Advantages

  • Total Corrosion Immunity – Engineered to perform in highly aggressive chemical, marine, and industrial environments without degradation
  • Fire-Safe Performance – Fire-retardant resin systems available, compliant with ASTM E84 Class 1 flame spread requirements
  • High Strength-to-Weight Ratio – Delivers exceptional load-bearing capacity while significantly reducing structural weight
  • Electrically Non-Conductive – Eliminates earthing requirements and enhances safety in electrical and high-risk zones
  • UV Stabilized for Outdoor Use – Designed for long-term exposure to harsh sunlight and extreme Gulf climates
  • Low Thermal Conductivity – Minimizes heat transfer, improving personnel safety in high-temperature environments
  • Zero Maintenance Design – No painting, galvanizing, or protective coatings required throughout service life
  • Extended Service Life – Long-term durability with significantly reduced lifecycle and replacement costs

Accessories & Hardware

Our FRP cable tray systems are supplied with a complete range of compatible accessories to ensure secure installation, structural stability, and seamless system integration across all project requirements:

  • Standard couplers and expansion couplers – for joint continuity and thermal movement
  • Angle plates and adjustable splice plates – for structural alignment and connection flexibility
  • Hold-down clips and clamps (FRP / stainless steel) – for secure tray fixing
  • Cover clamps – for cable protection and system safety
  • Trefoil clamps – for organized cable grouping
  • Divider strips – for cable segregation within trays
  • Blind plates – for end closure and system protection
  • Stainless steel fish plates and fastening hardware – for durable connections

All accessories are engineered to match the corrosion resistance, strength, and durability of the FRP system, ensuring long-term performance in demanding industrial environments.

Typical Applications

Engineered for Safe and Reliable Cable Management in Industrial Environments

Energy & Power Infrastructure

Includes: Oil & Gas Plants, Petrochemical Facilities, Offshore Platforms, Power Generation Facilities.

FRP cable trays provide critical dielectric security for high-voltage energy zones. Their non-conductive properties eliminate earthing requirements near substations and power plants, while their total immunity to aggressive chemical atmospheres ensures long-term structural integrity in oil and gas refineries.

Water & Environmental Management

Includes: Desalination Plants, Water & Wastewater Treatment, Marine & Coastal Structures.

In moisture-intensive desalination and wastewater facilities, traditional metal trays suffer from rapid oxidation. FRP systems are engineered for constant saturation and saline exposure, offering a maintenance-free cable management solution that never requires repainting or corrosion protection.

Industrial Processing & Logistics

Includes: Industrial Platforms, Walkways, Equipment Access Areas, Material Handling Systems.

The lightweight nature of FRP cable trays allows for rapid installation in complex industrial layouts without heavy lifting equipment. Their high strength-to-weight ratio ensures they can support substantial cable loads while remaining completely resistant to industrial chemical leaks.

Infrastructure & Utility Projects

Includes: Tunnels, Bridge Services, Railway Infrastructure, Public Utility Chambers & Service Projects.

As a fire-retardant and smoke-safe material, FRP is the preferred choice for underground utility tunnels and public infrastructure. Our systems deliver a reliable service life of 30+ years in subterranean and exposed utility zones with zero maintenance requirement.
