Product Overview

Engineered for GCC Conditions. Built for Long-Term Safety.

FRP / GRP Handrail Systems are manufactured using advanced pultrusion technology, producing high-strength structural profiles with continuous glass fiber reinforcement and chemically resistant resin systems. Designed specifically for harsh Gulf environments, these systems deliver exceptional durability under extreme heat, UV exposure, humidity, and aggressive industrial conditions.

Unlike conventional steel systems, FRP handrails do not corrode, rust, or require repainting, making them the preferred solution for oil & gas, desalination, marine, and infrastructure projects across Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC region.

Hardware SS 316L Stainless Steel

Handrail Configurations

  • Round Handrails (Ø50 mm) – Ergonomic grip, smooth finish
  • Square Handrails (50×50 mm) – High rigidity, industrial-grade strength
  • Custom Fabricated Systems – As per project drawings and site requirements

Key Advantages

  • 100% corrosion-resistant in marine, chemical, and saline environments
  • Designed for high UV exposure and extreme GCC temperatures
  • Lightweight structure with high load-bearing capacity
  • Electrically non-conductive and non-sparking (ideal for substations & hazardous areas)
  • Fire-retardant resin systems available for critical applications
  • Slip-resistant options for enhanced safety
  • Maintenance-free – no painting, coating, or corrosion protection required
  • Fast installation with reduced project downtime

Typical Applications

  • Oil & Gas plants
  • Petrochemical facilities
  • Offshore platforms
  • Desalination plants
  • Water & wastewater treatment
  • Power plants & substations
  • Marine & coastal structures
  • Industrial platforms & walkways
  • Infrastructure & metro projects

Resin System Selection

  • Isophthalic Polyester (ISO) – General industrial & water applications
  • Vinyl Ester (VE) – Highly corrosive chemical & offshore environments
  • Phenolic Resin – Fire-critical areas (offshore / confined spaces)

Installation & Assembly

  • Modular, prefabricated system
  • Fully bolted – no site welding required
  • Easy cutting and assembly using standard tools
  • Compatible with concrete, steel, and FRP structures
  • Quick installation minimizes labor and shutdown time

Safety & Compliance

Designed in compliance with:

  • BS EN ISO 14122-3
  • OSHA 29 CFR 1910
  • AS 1657
  • GCC / Saudi Industrial Safety Requirements

Design Criteria:

  • Guardrails required for fall heights above 500 mm
  • Standard handrail height: 1100 mm
  • Intermediate rail (knee rail) provided
  • Maximum gap between rails: 500 mm
  • Toe plate positioned close to floor level
  • Maximum post spacing: 1500 mm

Why FRP is the Preferred Choice in GCC

In Saudi Arabia and Gulf conditions, traditional steel handrails are prone to corrosion, coating failure, and high maintenance costs. FRP handrail systems eliminate these issues completely by providing a non-metallic, corrosion-proof solution that performs reliably for decades.
