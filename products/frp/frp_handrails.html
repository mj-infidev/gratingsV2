Product Overview

Engineered for GCC Conditions. Built for Long-Term Safety.

FRP / GRP Handrail Systems are manufactured using advanced pultrusion technology, producing high-strength structural profiles with continuous glass fiber reinforcement and chemically resistant resin systems. Designed specifically for harsh Gulf environments, these systems deliver exceptional durability under extreme heat, UV exposure, humidity, and aggressive industrial conditions.

Unlike conventional steel systems, FRP handrails do not corrode, rust, or require repainting, making them the preferred solution for oil & gas, desalination, marine, and infrastructure projects across Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC region.

Why FRP is the Preferred Choice in GCC: In Saudi Arabia and Gulf conditions, traditional steel handrails are prone to corrosion, coating failure, and high maintenance costs. FRP handrail systems eliminate these issues completely by providing a non-metallic, corrosion-proof solution that performs reliably for decades.

Typical Applications

Engineered for Safety and Durability in Industrial Environments

Energy & Power Infrastructure

Includes: Oil & Gas Plants, Petrochemical Facilities, Offshore Platforms, Power Plants & Substations.

FRP handrails provide critical safety for high-voltage and offshore energy zones. Their non-conductive properties ensure dielectric security near substations and power plants, while their total immunity to saline splash zones ensures long-term structural integrity on offshore oil and gas platforms.

Water & Environmental Management

Includes: Desalination Plants, Water & Wastewater Treatment, Marine & Coastal Structures.

In moisture-intensive desalination and wastewater facilities, traditional metal handrails suffer from rapid oxidation. FRP systems are engineered for constant saturation and chemical exposure, offering a maintenance-free safety barrier that never requires repainting or corrosion protection.

Industrial Processing & Mining

Includes: Industrial Platforms, Elevated Walkways, and Hazardous Processing Zones.

Specifically designed for hazardous processing environments, FRP handrails are spark-free and non-magnetic. They provide secure fall protection on industrial platforms while withstanding continuous exposure to aggressive acids, alkalis, and heavy industrial particulate matter.

Public Infrastructure & Commercial Access

Includes: Infrastructure & Metro Projects, High-Rise Maintenance, and Safe Rooftop Access.

FRP's high strength-to-weight ratio simplifies the installation of over-span access systems and public safety railings. The lightweight profiles reduce structural loading on rooftops and metro stations, while integrated UV stability ensures a long, maintenance-free service life in public spaces.
