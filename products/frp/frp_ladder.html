Product Overview

FRP / GRP Fixed Access Ladders – Saudi Arabia
High-Strength, Corrosion-Resistant Industrial Ladder Systems

Our FRP (Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic) / GRP (Glass Reinforced Plastic) Fixed Access Ladders are engineered for permanent installation in industrial environments across Saudi Arabia. Built with high-strength pultruded profiles, these ladders deliver superior durability and safety in corrosive, chemical, marine, and hazardous locations where traditional metal ladders fail.

Technical Specifications

Parameter Specification
Material Pultruded FRP / GRP
Process Continuous Pultrusion
Standard Width 400 mm / 500 mm
Rung Type Ø32 mm Fluted / Grit-Coated
Rung Pitch 250 – 300 mm
Stile Profile 50×50×6 mm
Ladder Height Custom per project
Safety Cage Mandatory above 3 m
Temperature Range −40°C to +120°C
Fire Rating ASTM E84 Class 1
Hardware SS 316L
