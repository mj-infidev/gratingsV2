Product Overview

FRP/GRP Pultruded Grating is a high-strength structural grating system designed for applications requiring long spans and heavy load capacity. It is manufactured using the pultrusion process, where continuous glass fiber rovings are impregnated with polyester or vinyl ester resin and cured through a heated die to form strong structural profiles.

Pultruded grating offers superior mechanical strength compared with molded FRP grating, making it ideal for industrial platforms, walkways, trench covers, and heavy-duty access areas in corrosive environments.

Pultruded Grating Profiles

Pultruded FRP gratings are available with different bearing bar configurations depending on load requirements.

I-Bar Profile

Standard structural profile used for most industrial applications.

T-Bar Profile

Provides improved structural stiffness and higher load capacity.

Heavy-Duty Bearing Bars

Designed for very high load applications and equipment platforms.

Bearing bars are connected using corrosion-resistant cross rods to ensure panel stability.

Typical Applications

Engineered for High-Performance Industrial Demands

Energy & Power Infrastructure

Includes: Oil & Gas Facilities, Petrochemical Plants, Power Generation, and Offshore Platforms.

Pultruded grating is the preferred choice for wide-span offshore platforms and high-voltage transmission areas. Its unidirectional fiber alignment provides maximum load-bearing stiffness, allowing for significantly wider support spacings. This minimizes structural sub-framing requirements while ensuring total immunity to saline corrosion and electrical conductivity.

Water & Environmental Management

Includes: Desalination Plants, Desulphurization Systems, and Wastewater Treatment.

Aggressive chemical exposure in desalination and wastewater facilities demands the ultra-high glass content found in pultruded profiles. These panels are engineered to resist high-pressure washdowns and constant chemical saturation, delivering a 50+ year maintenance-free lifecycle where traditional steel would suffer catastrophic structural failure.

Industrial Processing & Mining

Includes: Mining Operations, Industrial Processing, and Food & Beverage Facilities.

Designed for high impact zones, pultruded GRP provides an exceptionally rigid foundation for heavy machinery access and high-traffic walkways. The dense, pulled-fiber core absorbs vibration and impact energy without permanent deformation, offering a spark-free and non-magnetic alternative to steel in hazardous mining zones.

Civil Access & Structural Safety

Includes: Long-Span Walkways, Industrial Trench Covers, and Utility Access.

With up to 70% less weight than steel, pultruded grating simplifies the installation of over-span walkways and large-scale trench covers. The superior stiffness-to-weight ratio allows for safer, faster deployments without the need for heavy lifting equipment, while integrated grit or convex surfaces provide permanent slip resistance.
