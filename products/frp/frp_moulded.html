Moulded Gratings

FRP Molded Grating (also referred to as GRP – Glass Reinforced Plastic Grating ) is a high-performance composite structural grating designed for industrial flooring, walkways, platforms, trench covers, and access systems operating in corrosive and demanding environments.

The grating is manufactured using a one-piece molding process, where continuous glass fiber rovings are arranged in both longitudinal and transverse directions within a precision mold and fully impregnated with a thermosetting resin system. The panel is then cured under controlled conditions to form a strong monolithic composite structure without welding, mechanical joints, or metallic components.

Due to its bi-directional fiber structure, molded FRP grating distributes loads evenly in both directions, providing excellent structural stability, impact resistance, and durability. Compared with traditional steel gratings, FRP gratings are lightweight, corrosion resistant, and require minimal maintenance, making them ideal for long-term industrial applications.

Typical Applications

Resilient Access Solutions for the World’s Most Demanding Environments

Energy & Power Infrastructure

Includes: Oil & Gas Facilities, Petrochemical Plants, Power Generation, and Offshore Platforms.

In environments defined by high-stakes chemical exposure, FRP molded grating provides an immune barrier to corrosive gases and saline spray. Its non-sparking properties and fire-retardant resins ensure peak safety in explosive atmospheres without the catastrophic weight or rust-risk of traditional metals.

Water & Environmental Management

Includes: Desalination Plants, Water & Wastewater Treatment Facilities.

Constant submersion and chemical disinfection cycles cause conventional steel to fail, necessitating costly replacement within years. Our FRP solutions deliver a 50-year service life in these aggressively wet settings, offering zero-corrosion performance and superior mold resistance for critical utility infrastructure.

Industrial Processing & Mining

Includes: Mining Operations, Industrial Processing, and Food & Beverage Facilities.

FRP’s non-porous surface prevents bacterial growth in food-grade environments, while its non-conductive nature eliminates electrical and magnetic interference in heavy mining operations. These panels provide a hygienic, rugged, and spark-free foundation that withstands heavy impact better than rigid steel.

Civil Access & Structural Safety

Includes: Walkways, Access Platforms, Trench Covers, and Stair Treads.

Designed for rapid installation with minimal labor, FRP panels reduce structural weight loads by up to 75% compared to steel. Integrated concave or gritted top surfaces provide permanent slip-resistance, significantly reducing workplace accidents in oily or wet conditions.
