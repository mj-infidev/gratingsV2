Product Overview

We fabricate and supply ball-proof steel grating panels designed to prevent small objects — bolts, nuts, tools, and components — from falling through flooring systems, enhancing personnel safety and equipment protection in multi-level industrial facilities.

Compliance & Applications

Manufactured in Saudi Arabia and supplied across the Kingdom and GCC, our panels comply with Saudi Aramco, SABIC, SEC, and EPC contractor standards. Typical specifications include 24/100mm pitch grating or 25/100mm pitch grating pitch approved by the Saudi Electricity Company, ensuring reliability for demanding industrial projects.

Technical Data

Parameter Specification
Panel Type Press-Welded Steel Grating – Ball-Proof / Close Mesh Panel
Ball-Proof Criterion All mesh apertures designed so that a 35 mm diameter ball cannot pass through in any orientation
Mesh Pitch Range Close-pitch load bars: 24 mm – 30 mm (engineered for ball-proof performance)
Panel Supply Format Cut-to-size panels fabricated as per project drawings
Edge Treatment Binding bar or toe plate welded to all cut ends and cut-out edges
Load Bar Orientation Load bars installed perpendicular to the span of supporting beams
Cross Bar Type Twisted square bar
Cross Bar Sizes 6.0 mm, 8.0 mm, 10.0 mm
Standard Mesh Pitch (Load Bar × Cross Bar) 24×33 mm, 24×38 mm, 30×50 mm (typical for ball-proof panels)
Welding Method Resistance pressure welding (press-weld process)
Safety Compliance DROPS (Dropped Object Prevention Scheme), OSHA 29 CFR 1910.23 , offshore platform safety standards
