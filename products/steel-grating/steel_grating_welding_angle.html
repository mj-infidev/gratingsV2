Welding Angle with Grating

Home / Products / Steel Grating Product / Welding Angle with Grating
Back to Steel Grating Product
  • Steel Grating with Welded Angle Frame Steel grating panels with welded angle frames are designed to provide additional structural support, improved edge protection, and easier installation in industrial flooring systems.

The steel angle frame is continuously welded around the perimeter of the grating panel, creating a rigid and durable structure. This fabrication method ensures the grating panel maintains its shape during handling, transportation, and installation while providing a strong seating surface for support structures or trench openings.

The welded angle frame also helps distribute loads evenly and protects exposed edges from damage.Angle-framed gratings are commonly used in platforms, walkways, trench covers, drainage channels, and access openings, where extra strength and precise fitting are required

All panels are custom-fabricated to project drawings and site dimensions, ensuring accurate fit, reliable performance, and long service life in demanding industrial environments.

Key Features

  • Steel angle frame welded around grating perimeter
  • Provides extra rigidity and structural strength
  • Protects grating edges from damage
  • Ensures stable seating on support structures
  • Custom fabrication to site drawings and specifications
  • Suitable for heavy-duty industrial applications

Finish Type Description Typical Applications
Self Colour (Black / Mill Finish) Supplied in untreated carbon steel with natural mill finish. Suitable for workshop fabrication or on-site modification where additional surface treatment such as galvanizing or painting will be applied later. Indoor installations, fabrication works, temporary structures
Hot-Dip Galvanized (HDG) Steel components hot-dip galvanized by immersion in molten zinc in accordance with BS EN ISO 1461, ASTM A123, or equivalent international standards. Provides excellent corrosion protection and long service life. Outdoor structures, oil & gas facilities, marine environments, industrial plants
Stainless Steel Manufactured from corrosion-resistant stainless steel grades and supplied in standard mill finish condition. Provides excellent resistance to corrosion, chemicals, and hygienic environments. Chemical plants, desalination facilities, food processing plants, water treatment

Powder Coated Electrostatic powder coating applied to provide a durable and decorative finish with enhanced resistance to abrasion and weathering. Colour and coating thickness confirmed at order stage. Architectural applications, walkways, infrastructure projects
