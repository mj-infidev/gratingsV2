Product Overview

Hinged steel grating panels provide a practical and safe solution for frequent access points in industrial flooring systems. Hinges allow a grating panel to open easily without completely removing it, making them ideal for inspection openings, maintenance access, and service shafts.

The hinge assembly connects two grating panels or a grating panel to a steel angle frame, creating a secure pivot mechanism. Hinges are welded flush with the top edge of the grating to maintain a smooth walking surface and ensure safe operation.

Standard hinged panels open to an approximately 90° angle, allowing easy access to equipment or service areas below the grating. Larger opening angles of up to 180° can be provided depending on project requirements.

All hinged grating panels are custom-fabricated to project drawings, ensuring precise fit, structural strength, and reliable long-term performance in demanding industrial environments.

Surface Finish Options

Finish Type Description Typical Applications
Self Colour (Black / Mill Finish) Supplied in untreated carbon steel with natural mill finish. Suitable for workshop fabrication or on-site modification where additional surface treatment such as galvanizing or painting will be applied later. Indoor installations, fabrication works, temporary structures
Hot-Dip Galvanized (HDG) Steel components hot-dip galvanized by immersion in molten zinc in accordance with BS EN ISO 1461, ASTM A123, or equivalent international standards. Provides excellent corrosion protection and long service life. Outdoor structures, oil & gas facilities, marine environments, industrial plants
Stainless Steel Manufactured from corrosion-resistant stainless steel grades and supplied in standard mill finish condition. Provides excellent resistance to corrosion, chemicals, and hygienic environments. Chemical plants, desalination facilities, food processing plants, water treatment

Powder Coated Electrostatic powder coating applied to provide a durable and decorative finish with enhanced resistance to abrasion and weathering. Colour and coating thickness confirmed at order stage. Architectural applications, walkways, infrastructure projects
