Product Overview

  • GRATING CLAMP GI/SS
  • Grating Fixing Clips (Universal Grating Clips)

Grating Fixing Clips, also known as Universal Grating Clips, are the most reliable and widely used solution for securing open mesh steel gratings to supporting structural members without the need for drilling or welding. These clips ensure a strong, vibration-resistant, and maintenance-friendly installation while preserving the integrity of the grating and supporting structure.

Our Grating Fixing Clips are designed to suit various grating pitches and depths up to 60 mm, making them suitable for a wide range of industrial, commercial, and infrastructure applications.

They are supplied either as a complete assembled unit or as individual components, based on customer requirements.

  • Assembly Details
  • Each Grating Fixing Clip set consists of the following components:
  • M-Type Top Saddle Clip
  • Pressed Bottom Clamp
  • Slotted Hex Bolt
  • Square Nut
  • Installation Method
  • Lower the assembled grating fixing clip from above the grating surface.
  • Ensure the top saddle clip sits firmly on the bearing bars of the grating.
  • Slide the bottom clamp towards the beam flange or supporting structure.
  • Tighten the bolt from the top using standard hand tools.

The square nut locks securely into the slot of the bottom clamp, allowing quick and easy tightening from above—no underside access required.
