Ductile Iron Manhole Cover

Our **Ductile Iron Manhole Covers** are engineered for superior performance under heavy traffic loads. Manufactured using high-grade SG Iron (Spherical Graphite) Ductile GJS 500-7, these covers offer a high strength-to-weight ratio compared to traditional grey iron.

Widely utilized in municipal infrastructure, telecommunications, and industrial sites, our covers are strictly tested to comply with **EN 124 standards**, ensuring maximum safety and durability in various road and pavement environments.

  • High Impact Resistance: Superior load-bearing capacity with minimal risk of cracking.
  • Anti-Theft Features: Available with security locking mechanisms and hinged designs.
  • Corrosion Protected: Coated with high-grade bitumen or epoxy for long service life.

Key Performance Attributes

Integrated rubber gaskets (EPDM) or precision machining between frame and cover to eliminate noise and wear caused by traffic.

Ergonomically designed lifting keys and hinged mechanisms for single-operator access, reducing maintenance time.

Deep-embossed diamond or circular patterns on the cover surface ensure high slip resistance in wet and icy conditions.
