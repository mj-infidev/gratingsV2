Ductile Iron Gully Grating

Road gully gratings and frames manufactured from **Ductile Iron GJS 500-7** are mandatory for modern highway drainage. Our range is specifically designed to handle the high velocity and repetitive shock loads encountered in urban and industrial curb-side environments.

Equipped with captive-hinge mechanisms and precision seating surfaces, these gratings ensure rapid water evacuation while preventing component theft and reducing rattle. All units comply with **ISO 9001 quality standards and EN 124-2 load performance**.

  • Captive Hinged Designs: Self-locking and hinged features to prevent theft and unauthorized access.
  • Optimized Flow Area: Designed for high light-opening areas to prevent road flooding and debris blockage.
  • Low Profile Options: Available in various depths to match standard asphalt or concrete curb depths.

Safety & Installation

Cycle-Safe Design

Vertical slots are engineered to prevent cycle tire wedge while maximizing hydraulic efficiency—ideal for urban cycle lanes.

Hinged Frame Locking

Integrated hinges and optional locking bolts provide a secure attachment between grate and frame, ensuring components stay in place under high-speed wheel loads.

Bedding Procedure

Frames are designed with large seating areas to distribute load evenly into the bedding mortar, preventing substrate failure and frame rocking.

Material Standard

Strict adherence to GJS 500-7 grade ensuring the correct ratio of tensile strength to ductility needed for high-impact gully environments.

Engineered for Precision Drainage

Max Hydraulic Flow

Optimized bar profiles and openings to prevent surface water ponding on highways.

Robust Frames

Precision-casted frames with seating gaskets to eliminate metal-on-metal rattle.

Project Ready

Full documentation and CAD details available for municipal and road engineering specs.

Gully Castings for Every Grade

All sizes are available from stock in both D400 and C250 grades for immediate road-work deployment.
