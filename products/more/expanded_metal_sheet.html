Expanded Metal Sheet

The manufacturing of expanded metal involves a precision process where a single sheet of metal or steel coil is simultaneously cut and stretched into a pattern of diamond-shaped openings. Crucially, no material is lost during this process, making it a highly sustainable and efficient solution compared to perforated plate.

Once expanded, the material can be supplied with a raised surface for maximum traction or flattened for a sleek, smooth finish. To ensure longevity, the final product is often coated with protective finishes like galvanizing, powder coating, or specialized paints to resist corrosion and weathering.

  • Economic Efficiency: Lightweight and easy to install with zero waste during production.
  • High Versatility: Available in mild steel, galvanized steel, and stainless steel to suit varied environmental conditions.

Common Terminologies

Understanding the standard terms used to describe expanded metal sheets is essential for selecting the correct product for your application.

SWD & LWD (Pitch)

SWD (Short Way of Diamond): The distance from a point on a knuckle to a corresponding point on the following knuckle measured across the short way of the diamond.

LWD (Long Way of Diamond): The distance measured across the long way of the diamond-shaped hole, from knuckle to knuckle.

SWO & LWO (Opening)

SWO (Short Way of Opening): The short distance between the inner edges of the opening.

LWO (Long Way of Opening): The long distance between the inner edges of the opening.

Material Metrics

Thickness: The actual measurement of the thickness of the base metal sheet being expanded.

Strand Width: The amount of metal fed into the press between each stroke of the diamond die.

Open Area

Expressed as a percentage, this represents the total space occupied by holes in a given area. It determines the light, air, and sound permeability of the mesh.

Key Advantages

  • Sustainability: Produced with no material waste, making it an eco-friendly manufacturing process.
  • High Strength-to-Weight: Stronger than equivalent weight wire mesh due to its continuous, non-welded structure.
  • Anti-Slip Performance: Diamond-shaped openings provide natural traction, ideal for walkways and ramps.
  • Flow Permeability: Allows excellent passage of air, light, and liquid, reducing wind loading and accumulation of debris.

Applications & Uses

  • Walkways & Stair Treads: High-traction hexagonal and diamond patterns for industrial safety.
  • Security & Fencing: Durable barriers for machine guarding, enclosures, and perimeter security.
  • Architecture & Facades: Sleek aesthetic for building screens, shading, and decorative panels.
  • Outdoor Grills: Specialized "Braai" grids that are lightweight, easy to clean, and cost-effective.
