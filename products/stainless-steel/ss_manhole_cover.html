Stainless Steel Manhole Covers

Infrastructure Excellence — High-strength, BS EN 124 certified manhole covers engineered for total reliability across Saudi Arabia and GCC civil projects.

Back to Stainless Steel Products

Product Overview

Hidara Gratings designs and supplies high-quality stainless steel manhole covers in full compliance with BS EN 124 international standards.

Our covers are engineered for reliable performance, safety, and long-term durability in demanding environments, ensuring total structural integrity across urban, commercial, and industrial sectors.

  • Certified to BS EN 124 Global Standards
  • A15 to F900 Load Class Reliability
  • Corrosion-Resistant (AISI 316/304 Grades)
  • Single & Double Seal Configuration Options

Load Classifications (BS EN 124)

Class Load Capacity Typical Applications
A15 15 kN (~1.5 Tons) Pedestrian walkways, compound cycle tracks.
B125 125 kN (~12.5 Tons) Mixed-use zones, pavements, shopping parking.
C250 250 kN (~25 Tons) Service roads, road shoulders, utility corridors.
D400 400 kN (~40 Tons) High-traffic main roads, highways, industrial zones.
E600-F900 600-900 kN Airports, seaports, military & heavy process pads.

System Specifications

  • Standard Openings: 300x300, 450x450, 600x600, 900x900 mm (Custom Sizes Available)
  • Configuration: Recessed (Tile-in) or Solid Top industrial designs
  • Materials: AISI 304 / 304L / 316 / 316L High-Grade Stainless Steel
  • Sealing: Advanced single or double seal systems for water/odor tightness
  • Finish Options: Mill Finish, Brushed Architectural or High-Polish Mirror
