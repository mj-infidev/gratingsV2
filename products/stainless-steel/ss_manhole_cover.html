Stainless Steel Manhole Covers

Designed for Saudi Arabia & GCC Infrastructure Projects

Hidara Gratings designs and supplies high-quality stainless steel manhole covers in full compliance with BS EN 124 international standards, ensuring reliable performance, safety, and long-term durability in demanding environments.

These covers are classified into six load categories, from A15 (Light Duty) to F900 (Ultra Heavy Duty).

For most urban, commercial, and industrial projects in Saudi Arabia and the GCC, A15 to D400 grades are widely used, as outlined below.

Technical Specifications

Parameter Description & Options
Standard Sizes Available in common clear opening dimensions, including: 300 × 300 mm, 450 × 450 mm, 600 × 600 mm, and 900 × 900 mm
(Custom sizes available on request)
Thickness Manufactured according to project specifications and load requirements
Cover Configuration Recessed stainless steel manhole cover or solid top stainless steel manhole cover
Sealing System Single seal or double seal options available, based on site conditions
Load Classification Light Duty, Medium Duty, Heavy Duty, and Ultra Heavy Duty (Designed in accordance with BS EN 124 load classes)
Material Grade High-quality stainless steel: AISI 304 / 304L / 316 / 316L
Surface Finish Mill finish, brushed finish, or mirror finish

A15 – Light Duty (15 kN ≈ 1,500 kg)

A15 grade manhole covers are ideal for non-traffic areas where only pedestrian or bicycle loads are expected.
  • Pedestrian walkways
  • Residential compounds
  • Cycle tracks
  • Landscaped and utility areas

B125 – Medium Duty (125 kN ≈ 12,500 kg)

B125 covers are designed for light vehicle movement at low speeds, offering a reliable solution for mixed-use zones.
  • Footpaths and pavements
  • Shopping complexes
  • Car parking areas
  • Commercial developments

C250 – Heavy Duty (250 kN ≈ 25,000 kg)

C250 grade covers are suitable for occasional heavy vehicle movement, where wheel loads are higher but traffic frequency is limited.
  • Road shoulders
  • Service and access roads
  • Industrial utility corridors
  • Light logistics areas

D400 – Heavy Duty (400 kN ≈ 40,000 kg)

D400 is the most commonly specified grade for roads and infrastructure projects across Saudi Arabia and the GCC. These covers are engineered to withstand frequent and continuous heavy vehicle traffic.
  • Main roads and highways
  • Industrial zones
  • Ports and commercial yards
  • High-traffic infrastructure projects

F600 & F900 – Ultra Heavy Duty

F600 and F900 grade manhole covers are designed for extreme load conditions and critical infrastructure. These products are custom-engineered on request to meet specific project requirements and authority approvals.
  • Airports
  • Seaports and docks
  • Heavy industrial plants
  • Military and special infrastructure projects
