Stainless Steel Custom Fabrication

We provide precision stainless steel fabrication solutions designed to meet the requirements of commercial, industrial, and infrastructure projects across Saudi Arabia and the GCC.

Our fabrication process combines advanced machinery, skilled workmanship, and strict quality control to deliver products with excellent strength, accuracy, and surface finish. All components are manufactured using high-grade stainless steel to ensure long service life, corrosion resistance, and reliable performance in harsh environmental conditions.

Sheet Metal & Laser Cutting Capabilities

We offer precision sheet metal fabrication and laser cutting services to support a wide range of industrial and infrastructure applications. Our facility handles sheet metal fabrication up to 5 mm thickness, with laser cutting capability up to 10 mm, delivering clean edges, tight tolerances, and consistent quality.

Capabilities Overview
  • Sheet metal fabrication up to 5 mm thickness
  • Laser cutting up to 10 mm thickness
  • High-accuracy cutting and profiling
  • Consistent quality and repeatability
