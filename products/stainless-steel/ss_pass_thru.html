Pass-Thru Stainless Steel Gratings

Pass-Thru stainless steel gratings are designed to combine high structural strength, open area efficiency, and hygienic performance. This grating system uses flat bearing bars as the primary load-carrying members, while round cross bars pass through precisely punched openings in the bearing bars, creating a mechanically locked and highly stable structure.

This construction method provides excellent load transfer, high resistance to deformation, and an attractive, smooth finish, making Pass-Thru gratings suitable for both functional and architectural installations.

Technical Specifications

Parameter Description
Load Bearing Bar Depth Available in 20mm, 25mm, 30mm, 40mm, 50mm and up to 60mm
Load Bearing Bar Thickness 3mm, 4mm, 5mm and 6mm options
Transverse Bar Type Round bars in 6mm, 8mm and 10mm diameter
Load Bar Profile Smooth (Plain) or Anti-Slip (Serrated)
Base Material Stainless Steel Grades AISI 304, 304L, 316 and 316L
Surface Treatment As-fabricated (Mill), Brushed, High-polish (Mirror) or Electro-polished finish

Design & Performance Features

  • Flat bearing bars with through-inserted round cross bars
  • Strong mechanical interlocking without distortion
  • High open area for drainage, ventilation, and light penetration
  • Flexible spacing options to suit safety and load requirements
  • Plain or anti-slip (serrated) walking surfaces
  • Suitable for pedestrian, equipment, and light vehicle traffic

Material Grades

Manufactured in high-quality stainless steel grades:

  • AISI 304 / 304L
  • AISI 316 / 316L
These materials provide excellent resistance to corrosion, moisture, chemicals, and cleaning agents, making them ideal for hygienic and outdoor environments.

Surface Finishes

  • As-manufactured (raw finish)
  • Pickled and passivated
  • Electro-polished
  • Sand or glass bead blasted

Typical Applications

  • Swimming pool surrounds and drainage covers
  • Pedestrian walkways and sidewalks
  • Commercial and residential developments
  • Food, fish, meat, and vegetable processing facilities
  • Markets and hygienic environments
  • Agricultural and cattle rearing facilities
  • Heavy-duty storage and racking systems
  • Fencing, partitions, and security panels
  • HVAC air intake and ventilation grilles

Supply Scope

  • Custom-fabricated grating panels
  • Fixing clips and fasteners
  • Framed panels with edge angles and stiffeners
  • Cut-to-size panels as per drawings
