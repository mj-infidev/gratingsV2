Product Overview

Wedge wire gratings and screens are precision-engineered solutions designed for efficient drainage, filtration, and screening.

Their distinctive V-shaped wire profile creates a self-cleaning surface, minimizing clogging while ensuring consistent flow and excellent structural stability. Ideal for pedestrian-friendly zones and process industries.

  • V-Profile for Superior Self-Cleaning
  • Wheelchair & Foot-Friendly Design
  • High-Strength with Precision Openings
  • Corrosion & Impact Resistant (AISI 316/304)
