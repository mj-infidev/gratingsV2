Press-Locked Stainless Steel Gratings

Press-locked gratings are produced using precision-cut flat bars assembled through a high-pressure locking process. The bearing bars are accurately slotted, allowing the cross bars to be mechanically pressed into position, creating a rigid, maintenance-free panel without welding.

This manufacturing method provides excellent dimensional accuracy and allows complete freedom in grating design.

Technical Specifications

Parameter Specification
Load Bar Height 20mm, 25mm, 30mm, 40mm, 50mm up to 60mm
Load Bar Thickness 3mm, 4mm, 5mm & 6mm
Cross Bar Height 10mm, 12mm, 15mm, 20mm, 25mm, 30mm, 40mm, 50mm up to 60mm
Cross Bar Thickness 3mm, 4mm, 5mm & 6mm
Load Bar Type Plain or Serrated
Material Grade Stainless Steel AISI 304 / 304L / 316 / 316L
Surface Finish As-fabricated (Mill), Brushed, High-polish (Mirror) or Electro-polished finish

Performance Advantages

  • Wide flexibility in spacing and panel layout
  • Custom bearing bar depth and thickness options
  • High load-carrying capacity with uniform weight distribution
  • Smooth or slip-resistant surface options
  • Suitable for both functional and architectural applications
  • Capability to produce symmetrical bar layouts for special designs
Press-locked gratings are especially suited for environments exposed to heat, moisture, chemicals, and aggressive corrosion.

Stainless Steel Grades

  • AISI 304 / 304L
  • AISI 316 / 316L
These materials provide outstanding resistance to corrosion and high temperatures, making them ideal for offshore facilities, food and beverage plants, chemical processing units, water treatment installations, and marine environments.

Design Options & Technical Capability

  • Multiple mesh configurations available
  • Bearing bar sizes manufactured up to 150 mm depth
  • Plain or serrated walking surfaces
  • Precision-fabricated panels to customer drawings
  • Tight tolerances for consistent fit and finish

Surface Finishes

  • Mill finish (as manufactured)
  • Pickled and passivated
  • Electro-polished
  • Sand or glass bead blasted
