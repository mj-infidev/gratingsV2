Aluminum Swaged Gratings

Aluminum Swaged Gratings are manufactured without welding between the load bars and cross bars. Instead, the cross bars are hydraulically swaged under high pressure into pre-punched load bars, creating a permanent mechanical bond.

This advanced swaging process ensures exceptional structural integrity, uniform appearance, and long-term durability. Aluminum swaged gratings are supplied in standard panels as well as tailor-made sizes, precisely engineered to meet project-specific load and span requirements.

Technical Specifications

Parameter Specification
Standard Panel Sizes 6000 × 1000 mm / 3000 × 1000 mm / Custom
Material Finish Mill Finish, Pickled, Anodised, Powder Coated
Material Grades EN AW 6060 / 6063 T6 / 6082 T6
Flat Type Plain / Mechanically Serrated (Anti-Slip)
Load Bar (Bearing Bar) Height: 20–50 mm | Thickness: 2–6 mm
Cross Bar (Traverse Bar) 10 × 3 mm / 12 × 3 mm
Mesh Sizes (Pitch) 30×50, 30×100, 40×50, 40×100, 13.5×100, 17.5×50, 17.5×100, 35×50, 35×100 mm
Bonding Method Hydraulic High-Pressure Swaging (No-Weld)
