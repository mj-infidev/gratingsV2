Aluminium Access Cover Systems

Our access hatch covers are engineered for safety, durability, and long-term performance in roofs, floors, and water tank applications. Designed for easy installation and reliable operation, they are trusted across industrial, commercial, and infrastructure projects.

Available in standard dimensions or custom sizes, our hatch covers provide the perfect solution for every access requirement.

Surface Finishes

  • Mill Finish – natural aluminum elegance
  • Anodized Finish – superior corrosion protection
  • Powder Coated Finish – durable, color-customizable surface
Each finish enhances appearance, durability, and longevity, ensuring your hatch covers remain functional and visually appealing for years.

Key Advantages

  • Robust construction for long-lasting strength and reliability
  • Superior corrosion resistance for harsh environments
  • Easy installation with minimal maintenance
  • Customizable sizes to suit project-specific needs
  • Clean, professional finish for industrial and architectural applications

Types of Hatch Covers

  • Roof Type – durable access for rooftops
  • Up-Stand Type – elevated frames for structural integration
  • Flushed Type – seamless floor integration
  • Glazed Type – transparent or semi-transparent access
  • Drop-in Type – quick-install and low-maintenance solution

Premium Materials

  • Aluminum 6063 – lightweight, strong, corrosion-resistant
  • Aluminum 6082 – high-strength alloy for critical applications
  • Stainless Steel – robust and maintenance-free for extreme environments
