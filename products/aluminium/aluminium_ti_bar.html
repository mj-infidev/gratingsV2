T & I Bar Gratings

T & I Bar Gratings are manufactured using extruded aluminium bearing bars in T-shaped and I-shaped profiles, offering superior strength, rigidity, and a high strength-to-weight ratio.

In this grating system, the cross bars pass through the center of the load bars, forming a strong interlocked structure without welding, ensuring long-term durability and dimensional accuracy. These gratings are ideal for industrial, marine, and architectural applications, where lightweight, corrosion resistance, and clean appearance are essential.

Technical Specifications

Parameter Specification
Standard Panel Sizes 6000 × 1000 mm / 3000 × 1000 mm / Custom
Material Finish Mill Finish, Pickled, Anodised, Powder Coated
Material Grades EN AW 6060 / 6063 T6 / 6082 T6
Bearing Bar Type (Load Bar) Extruded T Bar / Extruded I Bar
Bearing Bar Size Height: 20–50 mm | Thickness: As per extrusion profile

Mesh Sizes (Pitch) 30×50, 30×100, 40×50, 40×100, 13.5×100, 17.5×50, 17.5×100, 35×50, 35×100 mm
