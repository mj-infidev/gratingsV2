Aluminium Flat Bar Gratings

Traditional & Proven Grating Solutions — Engineered for Strength, Durability, and Lightweight Performance in Diverse Mesh Configurations.

Back to Aluminium Product

Product Overview

Aluminium flat bar gratings are a proven and traditional grating solution, engineered for strength, durability, and lightweight performance.

These gratings can be manufactured in multiple configurations, including flat load bars combined with flat or round cross bars, and a wide range of mesh sizes to suit different structural and architectural requirements.

  • Custom Designed & Fabricated to Project Specs
  • Lightweight High-Strength Construction
  • Excellent Corrosion Resistance in Coastal Zones
  • Multiple Mesh & Bearing Bar Configurations

Technical Specifications

Parameter Specification
Material Grades EN AW 6060 / 6063 T6 / 6082 T6
Surface Finishes Mill Finish, Anodised, Powder Coated, Pickled
Flat Type Plain / Mechanically Serrated (Anti-Slip)
Bearing Bar Size Height: 20-50 mm | Thickness: 2-6 mm
Cross Bar Size 10 × 3 mm / 12 × 3 mm
Mesh Pitch 30x50, 30x100, 40x50, 40x100, 13.5x100, 17.5x50, 35x100 mm
Custom Options Cut-to-shape and tailor-made modules available
